# Mouse artwork

Original unbranded RemTeh mouse illustration generated with the built-in imagegen tool at the owner's request. The interface animates the actual received button states over this illustration. The artwork is not a photograph of a connected or automatically identified device.

Official white RemTeh logo: unchanged existing brand asset from [remteh.by](https://remteh.by/images/logo_white.png), used on the mouse at the owner request. It is rendered as an exact overlay, not redrawn or AI-generated.
